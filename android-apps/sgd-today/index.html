About SGD Today

SGD Today is your Singapore dollar currency converter and exchange rate tracker - built for the way you actually move between currencies.

Check the SGD to MYR rate for the Malaysian ringgit, or run a quick USD to SGD conversion to size up a purchase in Singapore dollars. Planning a trip? Look up SGD to JPY and SGD to THB before you budget. Need to send money home or compare regional rates? Convert Singapore dollars to Chinese yuan (CNY), Indonesian rupiah (IDR), Indian rupees (INR), Bangladeshi taka (BDT), or Philippine pesos (PHP) without jumping between apps.

SGD Today keeps the rate you care about visible. Add a currency exchange rate widget to your Android home screen and your chosen pair stays at a glance. Search by currency name or code, save your favourites for one-tap access, and pull up gold (XAU) and silver (XAG) reference rates priced in Singapore dollars when you want a precious-metals check alongside your FX watchlist. Each reference rate shows its source and date, so you can compare it honestly with a bank or money-changer quote.

Exchange rates are indicative. Always confirm the final rate and any fees with your bank, money changer, or transfer provider - the app provides information, not a transaction.

Exchange rate questions

What is the Singapore dollar exchange rate today?

Choose a currency such as MYR, USD or JPY in SGD Today and check the date and source on its rate details. Each pair shows the latest reference rate available to the app, and the dates can differ by source.

How many Malaysian ringgit is 1 Singapore dollar?

Open the SGD Today converter, select MYR, and enter 1 in the Singapore dollar field. The MYR result shows what S$1 is worth at the app’s available reference rate.

How do I convert US dollars to Singapore dollars?

Select USD in the converter and enter the amount in the US dollar field. Read the result in SGD. You can also enter an amount in the SGD field to convert in the other direction.

Why does my bank quote a different Singapore dollar rate?

The app shows reference rates. Your bank or money changer can apply its own spread and fees. SGD Today’s 1%, 2%, and 3% spread examples show how a margin changes the conversion.

How do I add an SGD exchange-rate widget on Android?

Open Android’s home-screen widget picker, add the SGD Today widget, and select a currency such as MYR or USD to display against the Singapore dollar.

For published reference rates, see the Monetary Authority of Singapore’s exchange-rate table.
