Exchange rate questions

What is the Singapore dollar exchange rate today?

Choose a currency such as MYR, USD or JPY in SGD Today and check the date and source on its rate details. Each pair shows the latest reference rate available to the app, and the dates can differ by source.

How many Malaysian ringgit is 1 Singapore dollar?

Open the SGD Today converter, select MYR, and enter 1 in the Singapore dollar field. The MYR result shows what S$1 is worth at the app’s available reference rate.

How do I convert US dollars to Singapore dollars?

Select USD in the converter and enter the amount in the US dollar field. Read the result in SGD. You can also enter an amount in the SGD field to convert in the other direction.

Why does my bank quote a different Singapore dollar rate?

The app shows reference rates. Your bank or money changer can apply its own spread and fees. SGD Today’s 1%, 2%, and 3% spread examples show how a margin changes the conversion.

How do I add an SGD exchange-rate widget on Android?

Open Android’s home-screen widget picker, add the SGD Today widget, and select a currency such as MYR or USD to display against the Singapore dollar.

For published reference rates, see the Monetary Authority of Singapore’s exchange-rate table.

Privacy Policy

Last updated 17 November 2026

LDE Dev publishes SGD Today for Android. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app or email us for support.

Exchange-rate requests and settings

SGD Today requests Singapore dollar reference rates from Frankfurter, which provides a feed for Singapore rates published through the Monetary Authority of Singapore. It also fetches Currency API data through jsDelivr, with a Cloudflare Pages host as a fallback. The app requests public rate files over HTTPS using fixed feed addresses. The services and their hosting providers may receive your IP address and standard request details when serving those files. See jsDelivr’s privacy policy for its handling of CDN requests.

The converter uses downloaded rates to calculate on your device. Your entered amounts stay in the app and are excluded from rate requests and analytics events. The app stores cached rates, favourites, widget choices and settings on your device. Android may back up or transfer eligible settings depending on your device configuration.

Usage analytics and crash reporting

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how people use the app. Analytics can receive app opens, screens viewed, currency selections, changes to favourites and use of the converter and widgets. It can also receive an app installation identifier, device model, Android version and approximate region. Depending on the app and device settings, it may receive the Android Advertising ID where available.

We use Firebase Crashlytics to find and diagnose crashes. Google may receive crash stack traces, relevant app state, device information and installation identifiers for that purpose. Google receives the analytics and crash information through its Firebase services over encrypted connections.

See Firebase privacy and security information and Google’s Firebase Analytics data collection guidance.

Advertising in versions that include AdMob

For an app version that includes Google AdMob, Google may receive IP addresses, device or advertising identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostic information to deliver and measure ads and help prevent fraud. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Feedback and support

If you choose to give feedback in the app, it opens an email draft with your message and device manufacturer and model. You can review and edit the draft before sending it through your email app. If you send it, LDE Dev receives the sender’s email address and the contents of the message. We use that information to respond and investigate the issue.

Retention and deletion

You can clear the app’s local data in Android Settings. Any Android backup copies are managed through your device’s backup settings. User-level and event-level analytics data follows the retention settings in Google Analytics. Google says Crashlytics holds crash traces and associated identifiers for 90 days before starting to remove them. Support emails are kept as needed to respond, resolve the issue and meet any applicable recordkeeping obligations.

For privacy questions or to request access to or deletion of information we hold, email ldedevqa@bk.ru and include the app name. Analytics and crash reports use installation identifiers, so we may need more information to locate a particular record.
