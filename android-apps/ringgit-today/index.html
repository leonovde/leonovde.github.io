About Ringgit Today

Ringgit Today is your essential financial companion for tracking the Malaysian Ringgit (MYR/RM), with accurate, up-to-date currency exchange rates right on your phone.

It goes beyond the usual mid-market interbank prices. Ringgit Today shows data that reflects what you actually see in the Malaysian market and at local money changers on the ground - bridging the gap between official rates and the street-level exchange rates you encounter at booths across Malaysia.

Check the Malaysian Ringgit (MYR) exchange rate against:

  • Major global currencies: USD (US Dollar), EUR (Euro), GBP (British Pound), JPY (Japanese Yen)
  • Regional and neighbouring currencies: SGD (Singapore Dollar), IDR (Indonesian Rupiah), THB (Thai Baht), VND (Vietnamese Dong), PHP (Philippine Peso), BND (Brunei Dollar)
  • East Asian currencies: CNY (Chinese Yuan), HKD (Hong Kong Dollar), KRW (South Korean Won), TWD (Taiwan Dollar)
  • Oceania: AUD (Australian Dollar), NZD (New Zealand Dollar)
  • South Asia: INR (Indian Rupee), PKR (Pakistani Rupee), BDT (Bangladeshi Taka), LKR (Sri Lankan Rupee), NPR (Nepalese Rupee)
  • Other markets: CAD (Canadian Dollar), RUB (Russian Ruble), TRY (Turkish Lira), CHF (Swiss Franc), ZAR (South African Rand), SAR (Saudi Riyal), AED (UAE Dirham), KZT (Kazakhstani Tenge)

Ringgit Today includes these features:

  • MYR exchange rates sourced from the European Central Bank and Malaysia’s central bank, Bank Negara Malaysia
  • Best approximate exchange rates you can find at currency exchanges around Malaysia
  • A currency converter for fast exchange-rate calculations, working both ways—from and to RM/MYR
  • Daily silver and gold prices, plus the gold rate in Malaysia per ounce

The app covers all the popular Malaysian Ringgit exchange rate pairs, including:

  • Ringgit to SGD (SGD to MYR, MYR to SGD)
  • Ringgit to USD (USD to MYR, MYR to USD)
  • Ringgit to Euro (EUR to MYR, RM to EUR)
  • Gold to Ringgit and Silver to Ringgit prices per ounce (XAU to MYR, XAG to MYR)
  • Other local Ringgit pairs useful for locals, travellers, and visitors to Malaysia: IDR to MYR, INR to MYR, JPY to MYR, MYR to THB, and more

Exchange rate questions

How do I convert 100,000 Indonesian rupiah to Malaysian ringgit?

Select IDR in the converter and enter 100,000 in the rupiah field. If you’re working from a table that quotes MYR per 100 IDR, multiply that quote by 1,000.

Why is the money changer’s rate different from the app’s rate?

A money changer sets its own buy and sell prices and may charge fees. Ringgit Today’s buy and sell estimates apply margins to a reference rate. For a cash exchange, compare the final amount you’d receive.

How do I add a ringgit exchange-rate widget on Android?

Open Android’s home-screen widget picker, add the Ringgit Today widget, and choose the currency you want to display against MYR.

For the latest published rates, see Bank Negara Malaysia’s exchange-rate table.
