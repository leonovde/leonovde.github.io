About Ringgit Today

Check how much your money is worth in ringgit before a trip, a purchase or a visit to a money changer. Use the two-way converter for US dollars, Singapore dollars, Indonesian rupiah, euros and other currencies. Open a rate to check its date and view estimated cash-exchange rates.

Save the currencies you check most often or put one on your Android home screen. The app also lists gold (XAU) and silver (XAG) reference prices in ringgit.

Exchange rate questions

Is the ringgit going up or down?

The ringgit strengthened slightly against the US dollar at Bank Negara Malaysia’s noon rates. US$1 fell from RM4.091 on 16 November 2026 to RM4.089 on 17 November 2026. A lower USD/MYR rate means a stronger ringgit against the dollar. Open Ringgit Today for Android or pin its USD widget to check the latest available rate each day.

Is MYR getting stronger?

Against the US dollar, the ringgit was about 2.2% stronger over the five years to 17 November 2026. One US dollar cost RM4.1795 on 17 November 2021, rose to RM4.7935 in April 2024 during a period of ringgit weakness, and fell to RM4.089 by the end of the comparison. Track the daily USD rate in Ringgit Today or on its Android widget.

How much is $100 in RM?

US$100 was about RM408.90 at Bank Negara Malaysia’s noon middle rate on 17 November 2026. For a quick daily estimate, pin the Ringgit Today USD widget and multiply the rounded MYR amount shown for US$1 by 100. Enter US$100 directly in the app’s converter for the calculation at the latest rate available in the app.

How do I convert 100,000 Indonesian rupiah to Malaysian ringgit?

Select IDR in the converter and enter 100,000 in the rupiah field. If you’re working from a table that quotes MYR per 100 IDR, multiply that quote by 1,000.

Why is the money changer’s rate different from the app’s rate?

A money changer sets its own buy and sell prices and may charge fees. Ringgit Today’s buy and sell estimates apply margins to a reference rate. For a cash exchange, compare the final amount you’d receive.

How do I add a ringgit exchange-rate widget on Android?

Open Android’s home-screen widget picker, add the Ringgit Today widget, and choose the currency you want to display against MYR.

Figures and comparisons use Bank Negara Malaysia’s noon middle rates (CC BY 4.0). For the latest published rates, see Bank Negara Malaysia’s exchange-rate table.
